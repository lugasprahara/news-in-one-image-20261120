One-image summary of Friday 20 November 2026: a single 1024×1024 infographic carrying 31 verified stories from six continents — 11 illustrated panels (Russian missiles hitting three Kyiv bridges, killing at least five; Russia violating Polish airspace overnight; the COP31 draft dropping the fossil-fuel phase-out; Italy's general strike in 100+ cities; the UK–EU youth mobility scheme; the US Senate passing its energy permitting overhaul 62–33; markets plunging as the AI rally cracks; a pancreatic cancer pill hailed as a breakthrough; Bukele registering for a third term; Ethiopia–Eritrea border strikes raising war fears; and a Sydney man charged over a car-bomb plot) plus a schematic "Where it happened" map and 20 further briefs. The design is an original deep-teal, warm-paper and coral newspaper layout with hand-drawn flat SVG scenes throughout. Every story was checked against reputable reporting dated to that Friday — including an explicit day-check per story in SOURCES.md — and widely circulated wrong-day items (the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, the Trump–Xi–Putin Shenzhen summit, the Anthropic IPO debut, the GTA 6 launch itself and the Pete Rose pardon, all 19 November or earlier) were deliberately left out. The artwork lives in index.html and is rendered to entry.png; no external images or fonts are used.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
